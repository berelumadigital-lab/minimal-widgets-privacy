05 / Good to know

Questions?
Covered.

A few details before joining the Minimal Widgets beta.

Is the Minimal Widgets beta free?

The current Minimal Widgets app is being tested as a free app. Participation in the closed beta does not require buying a paid widget.

Can I install it right away?

Not necessarily. This is a Google Play closed test. You'll first need to be accepted as a tester and opt in using the Google account approved for testing. We will send installation instructions when access is available.

Will it work on my phone or tablet?

Minimal Widgets is being developed for Android, with layouts designed for phones and tablets. Please include your device model in your request so we can better understand compatibility during testing.

Does the app read my calendar?

Calendar access is optional and controlled by Android permissions. If granted, the calendar and agenda widgets can use on-device calendar data. See our privacy policy for the full explanation.

How do I contact the developer?

Email berelumadigital@gmail.com. For data-handling details, see our security and retention policy.
